## The week

Token demand ran the research agenda. Google's processing rose 330X in two years and Dell's own 2028 estimate, revised up 57X, is already being outrun by current volumes near 135Q per year. Every major forecaster has been wrong by 10-100X, and the driver is agentic and reasoning workloads that consume far more per request: multi-agent systems use up to 15X more tokens than chatbots. The fund's read is that inference infrastructure is now the dominant force behind the hardware trade, favoring companies capturing rising capex rather than bearing it.

That framing lines up with the week's buys. AAOI, LITE, and SITM adds sit on the networking and timing layer, with the fund pointing to CPO growth from $100M in 2025 to $39B by 2030 as copper hits physical limits at million-accelerator clusters. Lumentum revenue rose 90% YoY to $808M despite a 30%+ pullback from highs.

The SNDK add and the storage cluster tie to strong prints. Seagate posted record margins on tight nearline supply, revenue up 49% YoY with FQ1 guided to ~56% growth and nearline allocated into 2028. Silicon Motion grew revenue 127% YoY on boot drives, though three straight quarters of negative operating cash flow and a first-ever debt draw are worth watching. META was the exception and the exit. Revenue growth decelerated to 28%, operating margin fell 12 points YoY, and free cash flow dropped 91% to $784M as capex consumed 97.5% of operating cash. The new compute-selling venture while leasing third-party capacity read as incoherent, echoing the circular-capital pattern the fund flagged at Alphabet. The fund closed the position and covered its SMH hedge the same day.
